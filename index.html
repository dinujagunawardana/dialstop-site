How a round works

The game is built to be understood instantly, then get sharper every round. Match the needle color to the same color on the ring before it completes a full lap.

Watch the needle color

The dot on the dial shows the color you need to hit. The matching segment is always somewhere on the circle.

Tap at the exact moment

Stop on the matching color to score. Hit the small gold zone to earn a coin and bonus points.

Keep the streak alive

The dial speeds up, palettes change the vibe, and Game Center lets you compare high scores with friends.

Built for quick retries

Everything in Dial Stop is tuned around fast decisions, readable colors, and that one-more-round feeling.

Color-matching skill

The target color changes every round, so every tap is a fresh timing read.

Moving gold zone

Gold hits reward coins, but the prize zone moves inside the target arc.

Palette shop

Unlock clean, neon, cosmic, and premium palette styles with coins.

Continue option

Spend coins or watch a rewarded ad to continue after a missed stop.

Game Center scores

See friend high scores and invite more people straight from the leaderboard screen.

No account required
